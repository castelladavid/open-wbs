# Open WBS

Open WBS is an open-source desktop application designed to bridge a significant gap in the project management software ecosystem: simple, rigorous, and accessible scope management for traditional (waterfall) and hybrid projects.

Built with privacy and simplicity in mind, Open WBS is tailored for the academic community, students, and professionals who need a clean alternative to bloated enterprise software.

---

## The Problem We Are Solving

In traditional project management, defining the Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) is the foundation of scope baseline. However, project managers and students frequently face two extremes:
* Overly complex tools (like Microsoft Project) that force rigid scheduling constraints before you even finish defining your scope.
* A lack of dedicated, open-source tools focused purely on hierarchical scope definition, work packages, and clean educational workflows.

Open WBS cuts through the noise, providing a focused, lightweight, and completely local environment to design project scopes from the ground up.

---

## Key Features (Current MVP)

* Visual WBS Canvas: An interactive graphical workspace where you can create, move, connect, and disconnect project levels and work packages intuitively.
* Automatic Hierarchical Coding: Smart generation of standard WBS numbering structures (1, 1.1, 1.1.1, etc.) as you build your tree.
* Rich Node Details: Assign names, descriptions, and owners (work package owners) directly to each cell of your breakdown structure.
* Spreadsheet Export: Seamlessly export your complete hierarchical WBS structure into standard spreadsheet formats (such as Excel .xlsx or LibreOffice Calc .ods) for reporting and sharing.
* 100% Privacy-First: Runs locally on your machine. No cloud tracking, no mandatory accounts—your data stays entirely yours.

---

## Future Roadmap

As Open WBS evolves, we plan to bridge the gap between scope definition, cost estimation, and scheduling:
* CBS Integration: Linking low-level work packages to a Cost Breakdown Structure (CBS) for budget alignment.
* Activity Extraction: Transforming terminal work packages into actionable project activities ready to be integrated into Gantt charts and scheduling tools.
